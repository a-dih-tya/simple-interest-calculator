# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description

The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple project that calculates the interest earned on a principal amount over a specific period of time.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

* P = Principal amount
* R = Rate of interest per year
* T = Time in years

## Example

If the principal amount is ₹10,000, the rate of interest is 5% per year, and the time is 2 years:

Simple Interest = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100

**Simple Interest = ₹1,000**

## Features

* Calculates simple interest.
* Easy to understand.
* Useful for basic financial calculations.
